Every idea is tried in simulation before it flies. The shared AirStack simulator, built on Isaac Sim,
flies up to three Starling 2 Max drones on the same software as the real ones, and a strategy goes
from simulation to palm-sized Crazyflies to the Starlings. NUS and UPenn train their team strategies
in simulation, CMU's safety filter runs there too, and ST Engineering tests camera-only localisation there.
