Nothing flies until it has flown in AirStack's simulator. It runs on Isaac Sim and flies up to three
Starling 2 Max drones on the same software as the real ones. NUS and UPenn train their team strategies
in simulation, CMU's safety filter runs there too, and we at ST Engineering test camera-only
localisation there. After that, a strategy moves to palm-sized Crazyflies, and only then to the Starlings.
