The Strike vs Guard website brings together the programme's two strategy
tracks, the benchmark, flight results so far and the partners behind each layer
of the system.

Each partner maintains its own page, so news and results can be added directly
by the teams doing the work. If you are a partner and want to add an update,
see the contributing guide in the
[repository](https://github.com/Strike-vs-Guard/Strike-Versus-Guard/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
