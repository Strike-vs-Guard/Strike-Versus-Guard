Our paper STL-SVPIO, by Hongrui Zheng, Zirui Zang, Ahmad Amine, Cristian Ioan Vasile and Rahul
Mangharam, appears at IROS 2026. It uses how well a plan satisfies its signal temporal logic rules
to steer a set of candidate plans toward better ones, and it handles multi-agent tasks that need
synchronised timing; the paper is on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13333).
