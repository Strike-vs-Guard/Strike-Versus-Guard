On 16 November two Starlings flew the figure-eight that CMU's AirLab flies in Pittsburgh, in our
Singapore lab and on the same software. The eight is 6 m long and tilted, the two drones fly it in
opposite directions at 2 m/s, and so they meet head-on in the middle again and again. Nobody is on
the sticks.

Each time they meet, the safety filter eases them apart: one passes above the other, and both pick
their path up again. The ground station reported the filter correcting in five stretches during
the 23 s of the eight. The eight ends with the drones on opposite sides, so they land where the
other one took off.
