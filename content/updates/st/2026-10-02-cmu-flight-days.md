We spent three days in CMU's flight cage with the AirLab team. Three Starling 2 Max drones flew
with nobody on the sticks: they changed formation and flew across each other's paths to separate
goals, with the safety filter on the whole time. The NUS team strategy flew on the Starlings too,
its first run on full-size drones. The clips are on the [Flights page](../../../flights/).
