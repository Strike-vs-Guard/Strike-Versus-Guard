IROS 2026 was in Pittsburgh, so William Teo, Chen Jiaying and I went. CMU's AirLab hosted us, and
we showed Strike vs Guard at a demo booth with them.

We also brought two posters. William's asks whether scoring a multi-robot plan one pair of robots
at a time picks the right plan: on 6 of 7 maps it picked a worse one. Jiaying's asks when it is
worth taking another look before a robot decides where to search for an object.
